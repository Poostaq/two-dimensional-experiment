# Orc Identity

## Lore Hook

Orcs build legitimacy through maintained lines, fulfilled oaths, and the ability to keep fighting when a plan meets resistance. Their warbands are not uncontrolled hordes: drums, chains, banners, and scar codes turn physical brutality into a disciplined language of contact.

Within the coalition, Orcs are the bodies that make an opening permanent. They hold the dangerous slot, prevent escape, and give faster allies a stable point around which to act.

## Visual Read

- Broad silhouettes, heavy jaws or tusks, layered plates, chains, drums, and planted standards.
- Low, deliberate movement followed by forceful impacts.
- Vanguard plate, Reaver cleavers, Warden chains, Captain banners, and siege tools make jobs readable.

## Culture and Mechanical Promise

Orc status comes from responsibility under pressure. Mechanically, they establish contact, build consumable Armor, hold positions on the movement ring, and punish enemies that attempt to escape. Stun is rare specialist interruption, not routine control. Orcs do not use Bleed.

### Strengths

- Highest durable contact and Defense profile.
- Strong Armor generation, forced movement, and controlled finish windows.
- Reliable conversion of Poison erosion and Harpy isolation.

### Weaknesses

- Low Speed and limited self-repositioning.
- Kiting and action-order disruption delay their payoffs.
- Stun Guard prevents repeated lockdown.

## Positive Synergies

1. **Scrapshield Bruiser + Siegebreaker:** Goblin rotation delivers a target into sustained Orc contact while Armor absorbs retaliation.
2. **Fang Alchemist + Bonebreaker Reaver:** Defense Poison increases the Reaver's actual damage without duplicating Orc control.
3. **Storm Siren + Chainwarden:** Crosswind Pull isolates and moves a target, enabling Lockdown's moved-target requirement.

## Counterplay

1. **Kiting:** Repeated ring rotation and Speed control prevent Orcs from maintaining the same target.
2. **Control resistance:** Stun Guard and movement denial break the Chainwarden's decisive window.
