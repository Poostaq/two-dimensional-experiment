# Werewolf Identity

## Lore Hook

Werewolf packs read battle as scent, posture, breath, and blood. Their discipline is not formal like an Orc line; it is a shared hunt rhythm in which one hunter reveals weakness and another closes it. Recovery comes from continuing the attack, making restraint both their tactical weakness and cultural tension.

## Visual Read

- Forward-leaning silhouettes, long reach, visible claws, scar patterns, and moon or pack tokens.
- Sudden low-to-high movement when a wounded threshold appears.
- Howlers, wardens, seers, and stalkers remain readable through posture and carried ritual pieces rather than heavy gear.

## Mechanical Promise

Werewolves identify wounded targets, pursue them, execute, and recover through direct offense. Leech is their primary keyword, while Bleed supports tracking. Protection is interception and pack loyalty, not conventional healing.

### Strengths

- High Power and Speed with strong wounded-target conversion.
- Offense-dependent self-sustain.
- Excellent payoff after Goblin setup, Poison erosion, or Harpy isolation.

### Weaknesses

- Low Defense and risky commitment.
- Protection and healing can deny wounded thresholds.
- Rotation, movement locks, and peel break pursuit.

## Positive Synergies

1. **Wirefang Skirmisher + Moonfang Skirmisher:** Advantage turns Moonfang Finish into a stronger execution window.
2. **Venom Saurian + Duskhide Ravager:** Power Poison lowers retaliation while the Ravager sustains through direct damage.
3. **Gale Scout + Bloodtrail Stalker:** Successful isolation grants Advantage and lets Follow the Trail reach the exposed target.

## Counterplay

1. **Threshold denial:** Guard, shields, and healing keep targets above half HP and disable execution skills.
2. **Peel:** Forced rotation and movement locks separate hunters from marked or Bleeding prey.
