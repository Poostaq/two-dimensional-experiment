# Dwarf Identity

## Lore Hook

Dwarves are the coalition's endurance race. Their contribution to the relic's creation was not mystical theory, but exacting craftsmanship, metallurgical precision, and ritual engineering. When the relic failed, they understood the problem immediately: the item did not merely extend life, it imposed immortality without wisdom, self-control, or mercy.

This understanding shaped the dwarven culture of the present. They do not romanticize the relic or the old coalition. They see it as a warning that force without craftsmanship eventually becomes a weapon against everyone.

## Visual Read

- Heavy frames, broad shoulders, and dense, practical armor plates.
- Tool belts, guild marks, warding straps, and rune metal fittings.
- Thick hammers, axes, shields, and siege gear.
- Dense silhouettes that read as immovable and expensive.

## Culture

Dwarves value craftsmanship, lineage, durability, and certainty. They trust what can be measured, maintained, and defended. Their war doctrine is deliberate and costly: they do not seek beautiful victory, only reliable outcomes and unbroken front lines.

## Mechanical Promise

Dwarves are the coalition's front-line fortress. They win through endurance, equipment quality, and refusal to leave the battle line.

### Strengths

- Highest durability among the coalition races.
- Superior front-line anchoring.
- Excellent Armor and defensive support.
- Strong at denying movement and controlling contact.
- Reliable in long, attrition-heavy fights.

### Weaknesses

- Slow and difficult to reposition.
- Weaker ranged mobility than humans or elves.
- Poor at speed-based chasing or highly mobile skirmishing.
- Vulnerable if the fight breaks their lane or forces them into poor positioning.

## Positive Synergies

1. Dwarven anchor + human tactician: the human opens and manages space; the dwarf makes it survivable.
2. Dwarven line + elven backline: elves attack from precision distance while dwarves hold the front.
3. Dwarven resilience + monster attrition race: the dwarf creates a long, damaging exchange the monsters cannot easily sustain.

## Counterplay

1. Dwarves are terrifying when their line is intact. If the player breaks their contact, their value collapses quickly.
2. Forced repositioning and disruption punish them more than it does humans.
3. Heavy weapons and defensive stances create clear openings for fast specialists and ranged burst units.

## Identity Safeguard

Dwarves must feel like a durable, item-rich anchor race—not like a second Orc line. Their fantasy is quality, endurance, and battlecraft, not pure brutality. Their identity sits between reliable front-line control and craft-based resilience.
