# Goblin Identity

## Lore Hook

Goblins survive in unstable tribes, tunnel crews, junk camps, and ruined borderlands by turning discarded materials and split-second opportunities into collective strength. Status follows usefulness: the Goblin who keeps the crew moving, finds the escape route, or makes a stronger monster land the decisive blow earns authority.

They join the monster coalition as its connective tissue. Alone, a Goblin is nervous and brittle. In a mixed warband, the same creature becomes a caller, saboteur, shield bearer, or scavenger who makes six individuals behave like a mob.

## Visual Read

- Small, sharp silhouettes and expressive, watchful faces.
- Oversized asymmetrical scrap armor, hooks, ropes, pouches, and stolen tools.
- Fast, low movement with equipment that rattles and trails behind.
- Class identity changes the carried junk: shield walls, wire bundles, trade packs, knives, or signal horns.

## Culture

Goblins value usefulness over pedigree, improvisation over craftsmanship, and pack survival over honor. Arguments are routine; betrayal that weakens the crew is not. Their leaders are organizers and dangerous nuisances rather than noble generals.

## Mechanical Promise

Goblins act early, alter sequencing, create Advantage, and turn allied follow-ups into more value. Their movement manipulates the six-slot ring chiefly to improve action order and coalition setups, not to isolate targets better than Harpies.

### Strengths

- Highest concentration of sequencing and coalition setup.
- High Speed, flexible movement, disruption, and low-cost support.
- Strong mixed-race follow-ups and recovery from imperfect positions.

### Weaknesses

- Low Health and Defense.
- Weak fair trades and poor performance while isolated.
- Area pressure and anti-movement can break multi-unit sequences.
- Setup loses value when the intended converter is defeated or controlled.

## Positive Synergies

1. **Wirefang Skirmisher + Bonebreaker Reaver:** Wire Cut grants the Reaver Advantage; Break Formation consumes it to punish a contacted target.
2. **Mobcaller + Moonfang Skirmisher:** Point and Yell prepares a wounded enemy sequence; Moonfang Finish converts it into execution and Leech.
3. **Scrapbroker + Venom Saurian:** Emergency Parts protects the toxin specialist from control while Hand-Me-Down sustains Poison ramp time.

## Counterplay

1. **Area pressure:** Damage that reaches several adjacent ring positions punishes Goblins for maintaining setup chains and protection links.
2. **Isolation and anti-movement:** Preventing rotation or separating the setup unit from its intended consumer causes Advantage to expire unused.

## Identity Safeguard

Wirefang Skirmisher, Snarewright, Scrapbroker, and Mobcaller all contain explicit sequencing or coalition setup. Shivrunner also rewards a prior allied action. No non-Goblin race may exceed two such kits, ensuring Goblins remain the coalition's tempo/disruption glue.
