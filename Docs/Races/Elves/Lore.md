# Elf Identity

## Lore Hook

Elves are the coalition's precision race. They were the ones most comfortable with the idea that power could be made elegant, efficient, and permanent. Their contribution to the relic's creation was not brute engineering but a high-order magical and conceptual understanding of longevity and control. That same impulse led them to the most devastating part of the failure: they believed they could refine mortality into a controllable form and instead created an unending curse that preserved bodies while eroding minds.

The elf faction sees the relic not as a triumph but as a reminder that perfection without restraint becomes corruption. Their present-day hero party therefore fights with grace, precision, and clear tactical intent.

## Visual Read

- Tall, clean silhouettes with layered cloth and tailored armor.
- Longbows, staves, crystal focus items, and elegant weapon shapes.
- Visible magical effects: glyphs, runes, and refined focus gear.
- High-movement posture; they look like a race that can solve a battlefield before the enemy finishes reacting.

## Culture

Elves value mastery, timing, and long-form precision. Their war culture emphasizes elegance and strategic correctness. They are not built for the blunt front-line grind of dwarves or the broad improvisation of humans. Their strength is clarity of action and decisive control of the field.

## Mechanical Promise

Elves are the coalition's ranged and magical specialist race. They punish poor spacing, exposed targets, and uncomfortably wide formation gaps.

### Strengths

- Best ranged and magical pressure in the coalition.
- Excellent at target selection and tactical punishment.
- Fast, agile, and dangerous when space is favorable.
- Strong at punishing exposed or mispositioned enemies.
- Excellent conversion of good openings into decisive damage.

### Weaknesses

- Fragile if forced into prolonged direct contact.
- Lower front-line durability than Dwarves or Humans.
- Highly dependent on spacing and formation control.
- Vulnerable to disruption that closes the range too early.

## Positive Synergies

1. Elf precision + human tactical cover: the human creates the opening and the elf converts it.
2. Elf backline + dwarf anchor: dwarves keep the front steady while the elf controls the angle.
3. Elf control + monster exposed target races: they punish the monster coalition when it fails to maintain proper spacing.

## Counterplay

1. Elves lose badly if the fight becomes a direct, close-range brawl.
2. Pressure that denies movement or closes the distance punishes their strongest tools.
3. Their best attacks require room and timing; static or disrupted plays remove that advantage.

## Identity Safeguard

Elves must feel like masters of precision and range, not generic glass cannons. They are the coalition's elegant, tactical, and high-control specialists. Their value comes from the quality of their openings and the precise use of their range.
