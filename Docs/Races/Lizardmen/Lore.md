# Lizardman Identity

## Lore Hook

Lizardman enclaves survive wetlands, deltas, and sun-baked ruins through observation, measured preparation, and communal memory. Their toxin craft is neither frenzy nor indiscriminate cruelty: each mixture is selected for a precise weakness and allowed time to work.

Within the coalition, Lizardmen make difficult enemies manageable. They blunt Power, erode Defense, slow Speed, and build a battlefield other races can safely convert.

## Visual Read

- Low, stable silhouettes; scaled hides; controlled head and tail movement.
- Vials, reed weapons, blowpipes, scale shields, and sun-polished plates identify jobs.
- Deliberate animations contrast with sudden ambusher lunges.

## Mechanical Promise

Lizardmen select one stat axis, apply Poison, constrain options, and win through patient attrition. Every Poison source declares Power, Defense, or Speed; axes remain visually separate.

### Strengths

- Best long-fight attrition and anti-stat planning.
- Durable control bodies and precise debuff selection.
- Enables Orc pressure and Werewolf finish thresholds.

### Weaknesses

- Slow kill speed before stacks accumulate.
- Early burst can remove the applier before conversion.
- Defeating a toxin source early sharply reduces invested value.

## Positive Synergies

1. **Iron Tusk Vanguard + Venom Saurian:** Orc Guard buys rounds for Power Poison while reduced retaliation preserves the line.
2. **Mire Spitter + Moonfang Skirmisher:** Speed Poison delays prey and makes pursuit order predictable.
3. **Mobcaller + Fang Alchemist:** Goblin distinct-attacker sequencing converts Defense Poison into several meaningful hits.

## Counterplay

1. **Early burst:** Focus the toxin specialist before the second and third stack create efficiency.
2. **Axis switching:** Pressure a different stat axis than the Lizardman prepared to exploit, preventing one plan from converting every stack.
