# Harpy Identity

## Lore Hook

Harpy flocks read height, wind, and exposed routes as a social language. Prestige belongs to the raider who can split a formation without becoming trapped inside it. Their songs are signals and pressure tools, while hooks and wingbeats make apparently stable ranks rotate around a chosen victim.

## Visual Read

- Tall, narrow silhouettes; broad wings; hooked feet or tools; streaming feathers and cords.
- Clear airborne arcs follow the six-slot ring instead of teleporting between abstract positions.
- Sirens use resonant ornaments, Scouts carry markers, Raiders use hooks, and Nestguards spread shielding wings.

## Mechanical Promise

Harpies produce the strongest forced movement. They rotate occupied paths, change neighbors, and create brief exposure. Their Advantage requires a successful hostile movement or isolation event, preserving Goblin ownership of unconditional sequencing.

**Exposed** is an unnamed Harpy effect: after a Harpy forcibly moves an enemy, a skill may mark it Exposed until its next eligible action. Exposed does not stack; reapplication refreshes. The next qualifying Harpy or allied effect consumes it only if its skill says so.

**Isolated** is an unnamed Skyhook effect applied by a hostile Move 2 or Move 3 rotation. It lasts until the enemy's next eligible action and represents separation from its prior neighbors; it does not require empty slots.

### Strengths

- Best Move 2-3 access and occupied-path manipulation.
- Chooses which relationships in a full formation change.
- Strong setup for Orc contact and Werewolf execution.

### Weaknesses

- Low Health and Defense.
- Movement locks remove much of the roster's value.
- Static direct trades and area pressure punish commitment.

## Positive Synergies

1. **Storm Siren + Wirefang Skirmisher:** Crosswind Pull creates Exposed; Wire Cut sequences an Advantage consumer into the new position.
2. **Skyhook Raider + Chainwarden:** Snatch Away applies Isolated and moved-this-round, satisfying Lockdown setup.
3. **Gale Scout + Moonfang Skirmisher:** Spot the Straggler grants Advantage after displacement; Pounce converts it on wounded prey.

## Counterplay

1. **Anti-movement:** Movement locks reject Harpy paths before their damage, exposure, Advantage, and cooldown resolve.
2. **Prolonged pressure:** Guarded formations, area damage, and repeated direct trades exploit Harpy durability once Pivot cooldowns begin.
