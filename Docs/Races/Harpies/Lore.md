# Harpy Identity

## Lore Hook

Harpy flocks read height, wind, and exposed routes as a social language. Prestige belongs to the raider who can split a formation without becoming trapped inside it. Their songs are signals and pressure tools, while hooks and wingbeats make apparently stable ranks rotate around a chosen victim.

## Visual Read

- Tall, narrow silhouettes; broad wings; hooked feet or tools; streaming feathers and cords.
- Clear airborne arcs follow the six-slot ring instead of teleporting between abstract positions.
- Sirens use resonant ornaments, Scouts carry markers, Raiders use hooks, and Nestguards spread shielding wings.

## Mechanical Promise

Harpies produce the strongest forced movement. They rotate occupied paths, change neighbors, and grant Advantage only after a successful hostile movement. This preserves Goblin ownership of broadly accessible sequencing without introducing additional named states.

### Strengths

- Best Move 2-3 access and occupied-path manipulation.
- Chooses which relationships in a full formation change.
- Strong movement-gated setup for Orc contact and Werewolf execution.

### Weaknesses

- Low Health and Defense.
- Enemies changing occupancy before confirmation invalidates planned paths.
- Static direct trades and area pressure punish commitment.

## Positive Synergies

1. **Storm Siren + Wirefang Skirmisher:** Crosswind Pull changes enemy neighbors; Wire Cut sequences an Advantage consumer into the new position.
2. **Skyhook Raider + Chainwarden:** Snatch Away moves a target three slots, satisfying Lockdown setup.
3. **Gale Scout + Moonfang Skirmisher:** Spot the Straggler grants Advantage after movement; Pounce converts it on a wounded target.

## Counterplay

1. **Path disruption:** Changing occupancy before confirmation rejects Harpy paths before damage, Advantage, and cooldown resolve.
2. **Prolonged pressure:** Guarded formations, area damage, and repeated direct trades exploit Harpy durability once Pivot cooldowns begin.
